About This Website

India Peaks is a dedicated platform to explore and celebrate the stunning mountain ranges of India — from the towering Himalayas in the north to the lush Western Ghats in the south.

India is home to some of the world's highest and most breathtaking peaks. The Himalayan range alone spans across several states including Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, and Ladakh.

Our goal is to inspire trekkers, mountaineers, and nature lovers to discover and respect these natural wonders.

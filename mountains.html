Mountains of India

A complete list of India's most iconic peaks

Kangchenjunga

🏔️ 8,586 m | Sikkim

Highest peak in India, third highest in the world. Sacred to the Sikkimese people.

Nanda Devi

🏔️ 7,816 m | Uttarakhand

Second highest in India. Part of the Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve, a UNESCO site.

Kamet

🏔️ 7,756 m | Uttarakhand

Located near the Tibet border. First climbed in 1931 by Frank Smythe's expedition.

Saltoro Kangri

🏔️ 7,742 m | Ladakh

Located in the Saltoro Range near the Siachen Glacier in Ladakh.

Saser Kangri

🏔️ 7,672 m | Ladakh

The highest peak in the Saser Muztagh sub-range of the Karakoram.

Mamostong Kangri

🏔️ 7,516 m | Ladakh

Highest peak in the Rimo Muztagh sub-range of the Karakoram range.

Rimo I

🏔️ 7,385 m | Ladakh

Part of the Rimo group in the Eastern Karakoram, near the Siachen Glacier.

Hardeol

🏔️ 7,151 m | Uttarakhand

Known as the "Temple of God", located in the Kumaon Himalayas.

Chaukhamba

🏔️ 7,138 m | Uttarakhand

A massif of four peaks in the Gangotri Group, visible from Kedarnath.

Trisul

🏔️ 7,120 m | Uttarakhand

A group of three Himalayan peaks. First 7000m peak to be climbed (1907).

Kedarnath Peak

🏔️ 6,940 m | Uttarakhand

Sacred peak near the famous Kedarnath temple in the Garhwal Himalayas.

Sia Kangri

🏔️ 7,422 m | Ladakh

Located at the head of the Siachen Glacier, one of the world's highest battlefields.
